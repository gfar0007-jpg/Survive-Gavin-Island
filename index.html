How it started

It all began a day before Gavin’s birthday.

Gavin came to me and said he wanted to make a game. He loves Minecraft, and I grew up playing Old School RuneScape, so we sat down together, put both lists of ideas on the table, and turned them into one world with a bit of both.

We made the 3D models in Blender and Metasequoia 5, put together the game’s data files with a .dat maker, and wrote the code in IntelliJ with Java. The version you can download today is written in JavaScript and three.js. We work on it side by side, and every so often we sit down and hunt for bugs together.

Watching Gavin’s ideas show up on screen has been the best part for me. We’re still adding things and making it better, so please bear with us. It’s still in beta and it isn’t finished yet. Thanks for playing!

— Dad & Gavin

How to install

Download the setup file and double-click it. A setup wizard shows you what you are installing, asks you to accept the agreement, and does the rest. There is nothing to unzip. The game runs without internet.

Windows says “protected your PC”?

That is normal for small homemade apps. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Controls

  • WASD move · Space jump · Shift sprint
  • Mouse to look · left click break or attack · right click place or use
  • E talk or use · Tab backpack and crafting · T chat
  • K skills · J quests · N creatures · P settings
